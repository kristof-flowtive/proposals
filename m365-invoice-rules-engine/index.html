01 — Understanding

What you're solving for, and what fits the budget

You've designed the whole system: shared mailbox, Document Intelligence, a rules engine, Teams approvals with reminders, a Copilot Studio agent for supplier follow-ups, a REST write to your job management system, Dataverse and Power BI. You have a PM and a customer lead, and a hard date. The honest read is that $1,500 and 13 working days fit one of those six components well, not all six. The one that fits is the one everything else waits on.

Requirement

"Checks each invoice against purchase orders and agreed rates"

PO number match, then line quantity and unit price within a tolerance you set, then line rates against your agreed-rates table. Each failure is a named reason, not a yes/no.

Requirement

"Flags duplicates, GST errors and over-limit amounts"

Duplicates on supplier + invoice number, then supplier + amount + date window for re-sent invoices. GST checked at 15% against line totals, with the supplier's GST number present. Over-limit against an approval-limits table, which also returns who the exception routes to.

Requirement

"Python or C# Azure Functions, with automated tests"

Python, pytest, one fixture invoice per rule and per edge case, CI on every push. The suite is the spec your testing phase (to 16 December) runs against.

Requirement

"Daily use of AI coding agents, including running several in parallel"

Claude Code is my daily environment. Rule modules are independent by design, so agents build and test them in parallel against one shared contract while I review every diff.

03 — Rule tables

Your AP team changes limits, not code

Tolerances, approval limits and agreed rates are data the Function reads, not constants in Python. They can live in Dataverse tables once those exist, or a config file in Git until then.

  • ▸Tolerances: price and quantity variance allowed against the PO
  • ▸Agreed rates: supplier, item or service code, rate, valid from/to
  • ▸Approval limits: amount bands and who approves
  • ▸Confidence: minimum per extracted field before a rule trusts it

06 — Before kickoff

Three things I'll confirm on our first call

Q1
Where do purchase orders and agreed rates come from?
The job management system's API, a Dataverse table, or an export. Default: the Function reads them through one small adapter, so the source can change without touching the rules.
Q2
What exactly counts as a GST error for your AP team?
Default: GST at 15% of the taxable lines within a rounding tolerance, a GST number present, and zero-rated lines allowed only for suppliers marked as such. Your AP lead signs off the list on day 1.
Q3
Who is building the Power Automate side?
The contract is agreed with them on day 1 so neither side waits. Default: I join their check-in when the flow first calls the Function.
