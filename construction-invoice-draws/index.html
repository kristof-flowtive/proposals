01 — Understanding

What you're solving for

About 10 projects, 15–20 invoices each a month, all landing in Gmail. Your admin keys every one into Google Sheets, files the PDF, and at month end builds each draw request from your templates while checking nothing was already billed. Reading the invoice is the easy part to automate. Knowing which project it belongs to, and which draw it has already been in, is where the time and the risk sit.

Requirement

"Vendor, invoice number, date, amount, and project"

The first four are printed on the invoice. Project usually isn't. It's matched from the job site address, a PO or job number, or the vendor's usual projects, using a Projects tab you control. When the match isn't certain, the invoice goes to review, never into a guessed tracker.

Requirement

"Excluding invoices already included in prior approved draws"

Every invoice row gets a Draw column. Preparing a draw marks its invoices "pending"; approving it stamps the draw number. The next draw only picks up invoices with an empty Draw column, so nothing is billed twice and nothing is forgotten.

Requirement

"Some containing links to online invoices"

If the link serves a PDF, it's downloaded and treated like an attachment. If it opens a vendor portal behind a login, the email goes to review as "download manually" with the link, rather than half-read.

Requirement

"Detect duplicate invoices and flag missing or questionable information"

Duplicates are caught on vendor + invoice number, then on vendor + amount + date for resent invoices with a new number. Missing fields, low confidence and amounts far above that vendor's usual all go to one Review tab.

04 — Reliability

Built so no invoice is lost or billed twice silently

01

The wrong project

The costliest error in a draw. Below the confidence threshold, the invoice waits in Review with the suggested projects. Your admin's choice is remembered for that vendor and address next time.

02

The same invoice sent twice

Vendors resend, CC two people, or send a reminder with the PDF attached. Exact and near-match duplicate checks run before anything is written.

03

An invoice in two draws

The Draw column is the record. A prepared-but-unapproved draw holds its invoices as "pending" so a second draw can't take them.

04

A misread amount

Line items are checked against the total, and the amount is compared with that vendor's usual range. A mismatch goes to Review rather than into a draw.

05

Make or Google has a bad hour

Failed steps retry, then land in the Errors tab with the email link. The email keeps its unprocessed label so the next run picks it up.

06

Ownership

Built in your Make, Google and Claude accounts. If we part ways, nothing stops working.

08 — Before kickoff

Three things I'll confirm on our first call

Q1
How do your vendors identify the project today?
Job site address, a job number you give them, or nothing at all. Default: address first, then job number, then vendor history, with anything else to Review.
Q2
Which past draws are already approved, and where is that recorded?
The first automated draw is only as good as that history. Default: I back-fill the Draw column from your last approved draw request per project.
Q3
Does an invoice need approval before it's eligible for a draw?
Some builders only draw on invoices a PM has signed off. Default: a simple "Approved" checkbox in the tracker that the draw respects.
