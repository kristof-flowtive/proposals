01Understanding

What you're solving for

You've built the quiz and written all 16 emails. What's missing is the wiring: each archetype's tag needs to start its own sequence in Kit, sent immediately and then on days 2, 4 and 7, with each person's own scores in the emails so every sequence reads like a private conversation, not a broadcast.

The copy is ready. The risk is in the plumbing: the five pillar scores have to arrive in Kit as fields or email 1 shows blanks, the merge tags have to match Kit's real syntax, and someone who retakes the quiz or replies with RESET shouldn't keep getting emails meant for a different moment. Each of those is handled below, before the first real quiz taker arrives.

Your sequence document"Each is triggered by the archetype tag ScoreApp applies at quiz completion"

Four tags, one per archetype, applied from your score bands (15–38, 39–52, 53–63, 64–75). ScoreApp's Kit integration applies the tag; I check that every boundary score (38, 39, 52, 53, 63, 64) lands on the right archetype before go-live.

Your sequence document"Total quiz score out of 75" and five "Pillar … score out of 15" fields

Six number fields in Kit: score, energy, emotional, relationships, work, purpose. If ScoreApp's native integration can't pass the pillar scores, a small Make step reads them from ScoreApp's webhook and writes them to Kit, before the tag fires so email 1 never goes out with empty scores.

Your sequence document"Replace these placeholders with Kit's dynamic field syntax before activating sequences"

Every placeholder in all 16 emails converted, with a fallback on the first name so nobody gets "Hi ,". Each archetype gets a test send with real scores, so every merge tag is proven in an inbox, not assumed.

Your sequence document"A subscriber only ever enters one automation"

Kit's "enter only once" setting works per automation, so on its own it doesn't stop a retake with a different archetype starting a second sequence. A shared quiz-sequence-started tag, checked at the start of all four, means the first sequence is the only one.

Not in your document, but it protects the most important moment

Someone who messages RESET shouldn't get two more emails asking them to

Your sequences invite people to message RESET on Instagram or WhatsApp, and some will do it on day 2. Those replies happen outside Kit, so by default the sequence carries on. A reset-sent tag you add in one click in Kit stops their remaining emails, so the conversation you're having personally isn't interrupted by an automated one. Your sending domain is also authenticated in Kit on day 1, so these emails reach the inbox.
