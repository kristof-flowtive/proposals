01Understanding

What you're solving for

You've built the quiz and written the copy. What's missing is the wiring: each of the four results needs to trigger a different email flow, four emails long, over the 7 days after the quiz is taken, with tags and fields set properly in Kit so you can segment later.

The 16 emails are the easy part. What usually breaks a quiz funnel is the routing around them: someone retakes the quiz and ends up in two flows at once, Kit's first email repeats the results email ScoreApp already sent, or people finish day 7 and never hear from you again. Those are designed in below, before any email is loaded.

You asked for"a quiz in the Score app that I need to integrate with Kit"

ScoreApp's own Kit integration if your plan has it, mapping each result to its tag. If it can't set everything you need, a small webhook step through Make or Zapier does it instead. Either way every quiz taker arrives in Kit within a minute.

You asked for"4 results from the quiz, so they will trigger 4 different email flow"

One tag per result (quiz-result-1 to quiz-result-4) and one Kit automation per tag. When a tag is added, the automation removes the other three result tags first, so a retake moves someone to their new flow instead of doubling up.

You asked for"4 emails long each over 7 days after quiz in score app taken"

Each result gets its own Kit sequence of four emails. Default timing is day 0, 2, 4 and 7; if your copy assumes different gaps, it follows the copy. Email 1 is checked against ScoreApp's own results email so nobody gets the same thing twice.

You asked for"build out automation with tags, fields, etc."

Custom fields for quiz_result, quiz_score and quiz_date, so you can personalise emails and segment by result months from now. A quiz-complete tag at the end of every flow hands people to your main newsletter.

Not in your brief, but it decides whether the 16 emails get read

Your sending domain gets authenticated in Kit first

Gmail and Yahoo now expect bulk senders to have SPF, DKIM and DMARC set up for their domain. Without it, a new flow from an unwarmed address tends to land in spam or promotions. I set up Kit's domain authentication with you on day 1, a ten-minute job in your domain's DNS, before the first real quiz taker is enrolled.

03Kit setup

06Before kickoff

Three things I'll confirm on our first call

Q1

Which ScoreApp and Kit plans are you on?

That decides whether the native integration carries the result across or a small webhook step does. Default: native integration, Make only if needed.

Q2

What should happen when someone retakes the quiz?

Some quizzes want the newest result, others want people to finish the flow they started. Default: the newest result wins and the old flow stops.

Q3

Is there a main newsletter people should join after day 7?

Otherwise they sit tagged but unemailed. Default: a quiz-complete tag that your newsletter segment picks up.
