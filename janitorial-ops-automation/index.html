01 — Understanding

What you're solving for

The business is growing and the admin is eating your time. Quotes get answered late at night, cleaners get texted about shifts one by one, timesheets get chased, and clients get reminded to pay by hand. None of that is hard work. It is constant work, and it all lands on you.

The thing the brief does not say out loud: five of your six asks depend on the same piece of data, the job schedule. No-show alerts, clock-ins, check-ins after each clean, recurring invoices and the weekly summary all need to know which cleaner is at which site at what time. So that schedule becomes the spine of the system, and everything else hangs off it.

REQUIREMENT
Fast, human-sounding replies to inquiries

Website form, Google and WhatsApp inquiries all reach Claude within a minute. It replies in your voice, asks the few qualifying questions you set (office or home, square footage, frequency, postcode) and books the walkthrough into your Google Calendar.

REQUIREMENT
Follow-ups on quotes nobody answered

Every quote you send gets a row in the sheet. Day 2, day 5 and day 10 follow-ups go out by WhatsApp or email unless the row is marked won or lost. You never chase a quote by hand again.

REQUIREMENT
Alerts when a cleaner doesn't show up or a job isn't covered

The clock-in is the proof of showing up. If no clock-in arrives within your grace period after the start time, you get a WhatsApp message naming the site and the cleaner. A job with no cleaner assigned is flagged the evening before, not the morning of.

REQUIREMENT
Clock-ins and job photos collected in one place

Cleaners send "here" and their photos to one WhatsApp number. The photos land in a Google Drive folder named by site and date, and the clock-in and clock-out times land on the job's row. No app to install, no login to forget.

REQUIREMENT
Recurring invoices and payment reminders, synced with QuickBooks

QuickBooks stays the source of truth for money. Recurring invoices are created in QuickBooks from the client's contract terms; Make watches invoice status and sends reminders at the intervals you set. Nothing is invoiced twice.

REQUIREMENT
A flag to you when someone complains

After each clean the client gets a short check-in. Claude reads the reply. Happy clients get a Google review link; anything that reads as a complaint stops the automation and pings you with the message and the site, so a human answers within the hour.

The six workflows, one card each

Leads

Inquiry to walkthrough in one conversation

  • TRIGGER New form, Google lead email or WhatsApp from an unknown number.
  • THEN Claude replies within a minute, asks your qualifying questions, offers walkthrough slots from your calendar, books the one they pick.
  • AFTER Quote row created. Follow-ups on day 2, 5 and 10 until you mark won or lost.
Crew

Shift reminders and the no-show alarm

  • 6PM Each cleaner gets tomorrow's jobs by WhatsApp: site, address, time, notes. A reply of "can't" opens an uncovered-job alert to you.
  • EVENING Any job for tomorrow with no cleaner assigned is flagged to you.
  • START + GRACE No clock-in yet? Cleaner gets a nudge; you get a message five minutes later if still nothing.
Timesheets

Clock-in, photos and clock-out on WhatsApp

  • "HERE" Any arrival message stamps clock-in on the job row. Claude handles "arrived", "on site", "starting now" without a fixed keyword.
  • PHOTOS Saved to Drive under the site and date, count written to the row. A job with fewer than your minimum photos is flagged.
  • "DONE" Stamps clock-out, computes hours, marks the job complete. A Timesheets tab sums hours per cleaner per week.
Invoicing

Recurring invoices and reminders through QuickBooks

  • CONTRACT Each office client's terms (amount, frequency, day) live in QuickBooks as a recurring invoice. One-off home cleans are invoiced from the completed job row.
  • DAILY Make reads open invoices. Reminders at due day, +7 and +14 by email or WhatsApp, worded by Claude, never sent twice.
  • +21 Still unpaid? Escalates to you with the client, the amount and the history.
Clients

Check-in, review request, complaint flag

  • JOB DONE + 2H Client gets a one-line check-in. Office clients get it per visit at the cadence you set, not daily.
  • REPLY Claude reads the tone. Positive: a thank-you with your Google review link, once per client per quarter. Negative or unclear: no automated reply.
  • COMPLAINT You get the message, the site, the cleaner and the photos from that visit in one WhatsApp.
Weekly summary

Monday 7am, one message

  • READS Jobs done and missed, hours per cleaner, revenue invoiced and collected from QuickBooks, quotes open by age, complaints and errors.
  • WRITES Claude turns it into eight lines you can read on your phone, plus a link to the full sheet.
  • ALSO A one-line "all systems ran" heartbeat every morning, so silence is never mistaken for calm.

03 — Owner controls

Everything you might change lives in one Google Sheet

Make reads the sheet on every run. Change a grace period, a reminder time, a qualifying question or the wording of a check-in, and the next run uses it. You never open Make after handover, and if you move your schedule to a proper tool later, the sheet is the thing we swap out, not the logic.

  • ▸Jobs — one row per visit: client, site, cleaner, date, start, status, clock-in, clock-out, photos. The spine.
  • ▸Quotes — every quote sent, its value, last follow-up, won or lost.
  • ▸Cleaners and Clients — names, WhatsApp numbers, sites, contract cadence, review link sent date.
  • ▸Settings — mode per workflow (draft, live, off), grace minutes, reminder times, follow-up days, minimum photos, your tone notes and FAQs for Claude.
  • ▸Log and Errors — what the system did, and what it couldn't.

04 — Reliability

Built so it alerts you when something breaks, and stays quiet when it doesn't

01

Fails to you, never to silence

Every Make scenario has an error route that writes to the Errors tab and messages you on WhatsApp. A 7am heartbeat confirms yesterday's runs happened. If the heartbeat itself doesn't arrive, that is the alert.

02

No false no-show alarms

The cleaner is nudged first, at start plus grace. A reply of "running 20 late" moves the check instead of waking you. Only a second miss reaches your phone. Grace minutes are yours to set per site.

03

Draft mode before live mode

Lead replies and client check-ins start in draft: Claude's message is sent to you for a tap of approval. After a week of reading them, you flip the Settings cell to live. Crew reminders go live from day one because the content is just the schedule.

04

Nothing invoiced or reminded twice

QuickBooks owns invoices; Make only reads their status and records each reminder it sends against the invoice ID. A rerun after a failure picks up where it stopped, it doesn't start again.

05

WhatsApp's rules, designed in

WhatsApp only allows free-form business messages within 24 hours of the other person's last message. Shift reminders, check-ins and payment reminders therefore go out as approved message templates, which I write and submit for you. Replies then open a normal conversation.

06

Your accounts, your keys

Make, the WhatsApp Business number, the Claude API key, Drive and QuickBooks are all yours. I build inside them and hand over the admin logins at the end. If we part ways, nothing stops working.

07 — Before kickoff

Four things I'll confirm on our first call

Q1
Where does the schedule live today?
Google Calendar, a spreadsheet, or your head. Everything in phase 2 reads from it. Default: I build the Jobs tab from whatever you have and, if you use Google Calendar, keep it in sync so you carry on working the way you do now.
Q2
Is your WhatsApp the normal app or the Business app, and are you attached to that number?
Automation needs the WhatsApp Business API, and a number can be on the app or the API but not both. Default: a new number on the API for the system, your existing number untouched. Cleaners and clients save one new contact. Moving your current number over is possible if you prefer.
Q3
QuickBooks Online or QuickBooks Desktop?
Online has an API Make connects to directly. Desktop does not, in any way I would put my name to. Default: Online. If you are on Desktop, phase 3 becomes an export-based reminder flow and I will say so before you pay for it.
Q4
What does an inquiry "from Google" look like when it arrives?
Google retired Business Profile chat in 2024, so these are most likely phone calls, or lead emails from your profile or Local Services Ads. Default: lead emails parsed from Gmail. If most are calls, I add a missed-call text-back so an unanswered call still gets a reply within a minute.
