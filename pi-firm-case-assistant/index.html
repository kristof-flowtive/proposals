01 — Understanding

What you're solving for

Four people are carrying a caseload where the dates matter, the records come in slowly, and every file needs someone to remember to chase it. You want an assistant that helps manage deadlines, creates summaries, drafts emails and follows up with medical records, working inside Casepeer and your Google tools rather than beside them.

Your brief

"Manage deadlines"

Key dates in Casepeer become Google Calendar events with reminders, and each morning your team gets one email listing everything due in the next 14 days, overdue first. The AI never calculates a date; it only reads the ones your team entered.

Your brief

"Follow up with medical records"

Every open records request is tracked with the provider and the date it went out. On your cadence, a polite follow-up is drafted in Gmail with the case details filled in, ready for a person to send. After the third chase it's flagged for a phone call.

Your brief

"Create summaries"

A one-page case summary in Google Docs on demand: parties, incident, treatment so far, outstanding records, upcoming dates and next steps, written from the case data and notes in Casepeer and saved to the case folder.

Your brief

"Draft emails"

Drafts, not sends. In phase 1 that's records follow-ups; client status updates and other letters follow in phase 2 once the first drafts have earned your trust.

04 — Reliability

Built so nothing slips quietly

01

The AI doesn't do dates

Calendar entries are copied from Casepeer by fixed rules. If a date is missing or looks wrong, the digest flags it for a person instead of guessing.

02

A daily check on the sync

Each morning the digest compares Casepeer's upcoming dates with the calendar. Any mismatch is listed at the top, so a failed Zap can't hide a deadline.

03

Drafts stay drafts

Claude writes into Gmail drafts and Google Docs only. It has no ability to send, and summaries say "not in the file" rather than fill a gap.

04

Your accounts, your data

Zapier, the Claude API key and the Google files are all in the firm's accounts. Record contents stay out of phase 1 entirely.

08 — Before kickoff

Three things I'll confirm on our first call

Q1
Which dates in Casepeer count as deadlines, and who enters them?
Statute dates, court dates, treatment milestones and internal tasks may live in different fields. Default: we sync the date fields you name, and anything unnamed stays out of the calendar.
Q2
Are records requests logged in Casepeer today?
If yes, the tracker fills itself. Default: if not, your team adds one row per request and the chasing runs from there.
Q3
Does the firm email from Google Workspace, and how should follow-ups sound?
Default: Gmail drafts from a shared records mailbox, using your current follow-up letter as the starting wording.
