01Understanding

What you're solving for

You're paying for leads and then losing them between the form and the first reply. Someone fills in the form at 8pm, nobody answers until the morning, and by then they've booked whoever called first. You want every new lead captured, answered fast, followed up until they reply, and a CRM that stays clean while it happens.

The part that breaks most builds is "stop as soon as the lead answers". That only works if the first reply and every follow-up go out through your CRM's own email or SMS, so the lead's answer lands in the same conversation and the CRM can see it. Send the AI reply from a separate inbox and the automation has to guess who replied. So Make does the thinking here: intake, duplicates, the AI reply, scoring, Slack and error handling. Your CRM does the sending and holds the day 1, 3 and 7 cadence.

You asked for"New leads from our website form and ads go into our CRM with no duplicates"

Before anything is created, Make normalises the phone number to international format and lowercases the email, then searches your CRM on either one. A match is updated and tagged with the new source; only a true newcomer becomes a new contact. A second form fill a week later is one person, not two.

You asked for"An AI step writes a personalized first reply and sends it within minutes"

Claude writes from the lead's own words (the service they picked, what they typed in the message box) and from a Facts tab you control: services, area covered, hours, booking link. It never quotes prices or promises a date. Typical time from form to reply: under two minutes.

You asked for"Follow-ups go out automatically for non-replies (day 1, 3 and 7) and stop as soon as the lead answers"

A CRM workflow holds the sequence, with a "contact replied" exit on every step. A reply by email or SMS ends it immediately, and the contact moves to Replied in your pipeline for your team to pick up.

You asked for"Hot leads alert our team in Slack. Everything is tagged and logged."

Claude scores urgency from the message ("this week", "leaking", "ASAP") and the source. Above your threshold, Slack gets the name, the job, the source and a link to the contact. Every lead gets a source tag and a stage tag, and every run is written to the Log tab.

Not in your brief, but it decides whether texts arrive

If the first reply goes by SMS, your number needs registering first

In the US, business texting from a CRM number needs A2P 10DLC registration, which can take from a few days to a couple of weeks; other countries have their own rules. The build starts on email so you're live in week one, and SMS switches on the day your number is approved. Opt-outs ("STOP") are honoured by the CRM and end the sequence.

04Errors and duplicates

Built so a lead can't be dropped silently

01

A step fails

Every module that talks to your CRM, Claude or Slack has an error handler: retry with a pause, and if it still fails, save the lead to Errors with the reason and post an alert. One failed step never takes the lead with it.

02

The same person arrives twice

Matched on normalised email or phone before anything is created, across the form and every ad source. The match result is in the Log so you can see why a lead merged.

03

The webhook fires twice

Each submission's ID is stored for 24 hours. A repeat is logged and skipped, so nobody gets two first replies.

04

The AI writes something wrong

It can only use your Facts tab, never quotes prices, and the first ten replies are checked by you before going live. If Claude is unavailable, a fixed fallback reply goes out instead of nothing.

05

A lead replies on another channel

Because messages go out from the CRM, an email or SMS reply lands on the contact and ends the sequence. A call your team logs can end it too, with one tag.

06

Something breaks at 2am

The Slack alert names the lead and the step. A weekly summary in Slack shows leads in, median reply time and anything still in Errors.

08Before kickoff

Three things I'll confirm on our first call

Q1

Which CRM are you on today, and on which plan?

The build fits GoHighLevel, HubSpot and most CRMs with an API. What matters is that it can send email or SMS from its own conversations and stop a sequence on reply. Default: the CRM you already use; GoHighLevel if you have none.

Q2

Should the first reply be email, SMS, or both?

Texts get answered faster, but need number registration first. Default: email from day one, SMS added as soon as your number is approved.

Q3

Which ad platforms send you leads, and do the forms live on the ad or on your site?

Facebook and Google lead forms connect directly; ads that send people to your website form are already covered by the form step. Default: your website form and Facebook lead ads in phase 1.
